## Level of Natural Language
- Human-like Understanding
	- Phonology(소리) -> Morphology(Word Parts) -> Syntax(문법) -> Semantics(의미) -> Pragmatics
- Raw Text Analysis:
	- Raw Text Character Stream -> Lexical Analysis -> Tokens & Leames Categorized Words -> Morphological Analysis
- Computer's Actual Process
	1. Input: `"I'm happy."`
	2. Preprocessing & Feature Extraction (Morphology & Syntax)
		1. Tokenization: `I, 'm, happy, .`
		2. Lemmatization: `I, be, happy, .`
		3. POS Tagging: `I: Noun, be: Verb, happy: adjective, .: punctuation`
	3. Analysis with ML Models
		1. [[Word Embedding]]: `I -> [xxx, xxx, ..., xxx], be -> [xxx, xxx, ..., xxx], happy -> [xxx, xxx, ..., xxx], . -> [xxx, xxx, ..., xxx]`
		2. Contextual Models (e.g. BERT, GPT)
		3. Task (e.g. [[Sentiment Analysis]])
	4. Output & Action

### Phonetics
- The physical aspects of speech sounds.
	e.g. The word "cat" is pronounced as \[kæt\].

### Phonology
- The linguistic sounds of a particular language.
	e.g. In English, the "ng" sound cannot be at the start of a word, but in Vietnamese it can.

### Morphology
- The senseful components of words (and other _tokens_) -- The building blocks of words.
	e.g. `unbelievable -> un- + believe + -able`

### Syntax
- The structural relationships between words, usually within a sentence (or a similar utterance).
	e.g. `[Det], [Adj], [Noun], [Verb]` / `The cat sat on the mat (O) / Mat the on sat cat the (X)`

### Semantics
- The meaning of single words and compostions of words.
	e.g. `The cat sat on the mat` = `A feline creature is resting on a floor covering.`

### Discourse
- Linguistic units larger than a single sentence, such as paragraphs and complete documents.

### Pragmatics
- How language is used to accomplish goals.
	e.g. `"나 추워."` => pragmatic goal = "창문 닫아줘" != 온도에 대한 이야기

## Tokens
- a wordform, a number, a symbol, or similar.
- The smallest text unit usually analyzed in NLP.
- Whitespaces are usually not considered as tokens themselves.

> [!note] 토큰의 예시
> Simple cases: "The", "the", "signed", "sigh", "42", "."
> 
> Complex cases: "i.e.", "42.42", "https://skku.edu"
> 
> Controversial cases: "is"+"n't", "42%", "oriental-based"

- Tokenization: The text analysis that segments a span of text into its single tokens.
	- Used in text mining as one of the most basic preprocessing steps.

### Morphemes
- The smallest linguistic unit with a meaning or grammatical function
- Corresponds to a character, syllable, word, or something in between.
	`cats -> cat + s`, `felt -> felt`

#### Morphemes vs. Syllables
- Syllables = phonological build blocks of words.
	`speaker -> speak + er (morphemes) / spea + ker (syllables)`

#### 의존형태소
- "cat"과 "cats"는 모두 혼자 사용될 수 있지만 "s"는 아니다.
- 이러한 형태소를 의존형태소라고 한다.

## Morphological Normalization
- Morphological Normalization
	- Identification of a single canonical representative for morphologically related wordforms.
	- Reduces inflections (and partly also derivations) to a common base.
	- Used in text mining to identify different forms of the same word.
- Normalization methods
	- Stemming: Identifies the stem of a token.
	- Lemmatization: Identifieds the lemma of a token.
		`derive -> deriv (stemming) / derive (lemmatization)`
		`am -> am (stemming) / be (lemmatization)`

## Syntax
- Syntax
	- The structure relationships between words, usually within a sentence (or a similar utterance).
	- Regularities and constraints of word order and phrase structure.
	- The syntax of a language is defined by a grammer.
- Structural relationships
	- Part-of-Speech: The class of a word is decided by its syntactic context.
	- Phrases: Sequences of words build meaning units.
	- Clauses: Grammatical units that express complete propostions.
	- Sentences: Grammatically independent lunguistic units.

### Part-of-Speech (PoS)
- Part-of-Speech
	- A lexical category of a word.
		e.g. Noun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, pronoun, conjunction
- PoS Tags
	- For analysis, more fine-grained(partly language-specific) word classes are considered, represented as token-level tags.
	- (PDF 예시 추가해줘)
- PoS Tagging: Assigning a PoS tag to each token.

## Phrases
- Phrases
	- contiguous sequences of related words, functioning as a single meaning unit.
	- Phrases can have nested phrases.
- Phrases vs. Constituents
	- Phrases: representing the constituents in the syntax of a sentence.
	- More or less, the two terms are used synonymously.
- Phrase chunking (shallow parsing)
	- segmenting a sentence into its top-level phrases.
	- Used in text mining as preprocessing (e.g. [[Named Entity Recognition]])
	- All phrases are also a by-product of constituency parsing.

## Clauses
- Clause
	- Smallest gramatical unit that can express a complete propostion.
- types
	- Main clause: Independent, can stand alone as a sentence.
		e.g. `I remained dry.`
	- Subordinate clause: reliant on a main clause and thus depends on it.
		e.g. `Although it was rainy`
- Clause recognition
	- Identifies the clauses of a sentence.
	- Not a common analysis.

## Sentences
- Sentence
	- gramatically independent linguistic unit consisting of one or more words.
	- Contains at least one main clause.
	- Many text analyses process a text sentence by sentence.
- Observation
	- There are infinitely many ways to compose words in sentences.
	- Yet, we can understand sentences we have never heared or read before.
- Sentence splitting (sentence segmentation)
	- segmentating a text into its single sentences.
	- one of the most basic preprocessing steps.

## Semantics
- The meaning of single words and compostions of words.

### Lexical Sementics
- The meaning of words and multi-word exxpressions.
- Covers word senses, semantic roles, and connotations.
	e.g. `bank -> 은행, 강변`
- Word senses
	- Distinctions in meaning between different uses of the same form.
	- Shared meanings between different forms.
		e.g. `bat -> 박쥐(동물), 야구방망이(운동)`
- Semantic Roles
	- Number of arguments of a predicate
	- Specific relationship the arguments bear to the predicate.
		e.g. `The girl gave the book to her friend. -> girl(agent), book(theme), friend(recipient)`
- Connotation
	- What word choice conveys beyond truth-conditional semantics.
	- includes emotional, cultural, and stylistic associations.
		e.g. `home -> 정감가는`, `house -> 좀 더 격식`
