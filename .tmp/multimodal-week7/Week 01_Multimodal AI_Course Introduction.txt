6 (i): The Real-World Problem
One event. Many signals.
• Imagine an autonomous vehicle 
approaching a busy crossing at night.
• A single real-world situation generates 
multiple, complementary (different but useful together) 
sources of information.
The challenge
Can one information source provide enough 
evidence to understand the situation reliably?
Real-world intelligence requires combining 
information that is distributed across different 
signals.
Think–pair–share
Which signal would you trust most in this situation?
When could that signal fail?
13

=== PAGE 14 ===
The Central Question
How does intelligence emerge when 
information comes from multiple sources?
Traditional AI systems often specialize in one information type:
• Computer Vision
• Natural Language Processing
• Speech AI
• Video Understanding
• Sensor AI
But real-world intelligence rarely operates on one source alone.
Key idea
The real world is multimodal. Intelligent systems therefore 
need mechanisms for integrating heterogeneous information.

Course Case Studies: From Theory to Real Systems
❑ ENVIRONMENTAL UNDERSTANDING SYSTEMS
1. Intelligent Baby Monitoring
Focus:
• Continuous multimodal scene understanding
• Detection of activity patterns and anomalies
• Early-stage event recognition in dynamic environments
20
Proposed baby behaviour monitoring framework. (A): Input is 
acquired from smart camera attached to RPi, (B): motion 
computation where two different algorithms are executed to compute 
motion of baby, and (C): control chart construction from the detected 
motion, abnormal breathing behaviour detection based on one 
interval of control chart, and alert transmission in case of any 
abnormal breathing or motion behaviour.
Hussain, T., Muhammad, K., Khan, S., Ullah, A., Lee, M. Y ., & Baik, S. 
W. (2019). Intelligent baby behavior monitoring using embedded 
vision in IoT for smart healthcare centers. Journal of Artificial 
Intelligence and Systems, 1(1), 110-124.
